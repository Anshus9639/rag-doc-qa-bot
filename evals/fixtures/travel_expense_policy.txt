1. Domestic Hotel and Meal Reimbursement
For approved domestic travel, hotel reimbursement is capped at INR 6,000 per night. Meal reimbursement is capped at INR 1,200 per traveler per day. Itemized receipts are required for both hotel and meal expenses; expenses without receipts may be rejected. These limits apply to domestic trips and do not establish a limit for international travel.
